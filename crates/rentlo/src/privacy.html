Rentlo privacy

Rentlo uses the details you share in WhatsApp to create your profile and recommend people looking for a room or a flatmate.

Your area and matches

Your private map shows an approximate area. Other members see selected match signals and broad area activity. A shared pin is stored for your profile and used at neighbourhood precision in matching. Share a neighbourhood or nearby landmark. OpenStreetMap supplies the street tiles for the area in view.

Access and contact

A private WhatsApp link opens your map and starts a browser session. The link is valid for 15 minutes and can be used once. Your name and WhatsApp contact are shared with a match after you both express interest.

Your choices

Pause matching from the map or by sending pause in WhatsApp. To erase your Rentlo profile, send delete, then delete yes when prompted. You can end this browser’s session using Leave private map.
